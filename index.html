    Evidence, with its edges visible

    Why these reminders exist

    The strongest case is simple: prolonged sitting is worth interrupting, and a short walk generally has stronger acute evidence than standing still. The exact perfect schedule is not known.

    Supported

    Move and vary the task

    WHO recommends limiting sedentary time and replacing some of it with activity of any intensity. HSE and OSHA recommend short, frequent changes of activity during computer work. Experimental reviews show favorable acute glucose and insulin responses when sitting is interrupted with activity.

    Useful, nuanced

    Micro-breaks and fatigue

    A 2022 meta-analysis found that breaks under ten minutes improved vigor and reduced fatigue on average. Performance varied with the task and break length, so Vulture Lite does not promise a productivity boost.

    Guidance, not a treatment

    Eyes, water, and coffee

    The 20-20-20 rule is widely recommended, but one small controlled study found no benefit from its exact 20-second timing during a short task. Water needs vary. Coffee is offered as a reason to step away, not as a caffeine recommendation.

    Primary reading

    WHO physical activity and sedentary behaviour guidelines HSE work routine and breaks OSHA computer workstation work process Physical activity break systematic review Micro-break systematic review and meta-analysis 20-20-20 timing study
    Safety boundary

    These are general prompts for adults who can exercise safely. Stop for pain, severe discomfort, dizziness, chest discomfort, unusual breathlessness, numbness, or loss of balance.
