# App surface brief

- **Mode:** Operate
- **Visitor success:** Start or maintain a quiet reminder rhythm, understand
  the next nudge, and act on it with minimal interruption.
- **Primary action:** Take the next suggested break.
- **Secondary actions:** Pause, snooze, tune the plan, or read the evidence.
- **First-visit action:** Accept the default notification-and-chime signals,
  choose a quiet start, or customize the balanced plan.
- **Critical truth:** Reminders are reliable while the page remains open;
  fully closing the browser ends static-site scheduling.
- **Emotional target:** Looked after, never watched.
